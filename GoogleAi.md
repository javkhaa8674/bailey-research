## 2. POINT OF INTEREST (POI) & LIQUIDITY RULES

* Swept Supply/Demand Zones: If a POI/Zone is swept/manipulated but price fails to hold past it, the zone remains VALID. Look for immediate counter-move setups.
* Liquidity Pools: Identify "Equal Lows" or "Equal Highs" building up below/above zones. Anticipate price taking out this liquidity into a deeper 4H POI before moving.
